Built around your brief

Start with the business outcome.

Whether you need awareness, content, trial, feedback or acquisition support, define the audience and action first. The campaign format follows.

  • Define audience and geography
  • Agree the campaign objective and deliverables
  • Set review, approval and reporting expectations
  • Confirm timeline, budget and measurement
Campaign planning principle

Clear briefs create better outcomes. Include the target audience, key message, content specifications, do's and don'ts, timeline, approval process and success metrics.
